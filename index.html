  • Python
  • NumPy
  • pandas
  • Open3D
  • Matplotlib
  • Plotly

It generates candidate alignments, scores each one on how well the overlapping geometry agrees, and selects the best fit. Measurement variation is rendered as heat map reports that supported inspection analysis and decision-making.
